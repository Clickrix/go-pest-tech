About us

42 years solving people’s pest problems

Lorne Hill is the founder & CEO of GoPestTech. He started his career in April 1985 doing pest management in southeastern Wisconsin, became supervisor of the Service Department — training and monitoring 7 technicians — and developed a comprehensive pest management program for the Milwaukee County Zoo.

“As pest management companies grow and especially if they grow too fast, the focus tends to shift away from the specific needs of individual customers and a ‘cookie-cutter’ philosophy develops.” That is why Lorne started GoPestTech — with the client and service always the No. 1 concern.

  • Purdue Pest Management Technologies — 93.8%, rated excellent
